Moonitaur privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Moonitaur is a Chrome extension for people who work on Moodle sites. This page explains what it reads, what it keeps, and what leaves your computer.

What Moonitaur reads

When you open Moonitaur on a Moodle page, it reads that page and makes requests to that Moodle site from your signed-in tab, as you. Moodle decides what you are allowed to see and change, exactly as it does on its own pages. This can include course details, names and email addresses of people in your courses, and site settings, depending on your role.

If you allow it, a small script checks every page you visit for Moodle's page markup, so the toolbar icon can turn green on Moodle sites. It reads two attributes of the page's body element and nothing else, and it sends nothing anywhere.

What Moonitaur keeps

These are saved in Chrome's extension storage on your computer only:

  • Moodle site addresses you have opened Moonitaur on, with the environment label you chose (local, staging or live)
  • Your notes about each site, and your pinned pages
  • Settings you changed with a timer, and their earlier values, until the timer turns them back
  • The Moodle version of each site and the plugin list, cached for up to a few hours
  • The last screen you had open in Moonitaur, for 15 minutes

Removing the extension deletes all of it.

What leaves your computer

Moonitaur sends requests to the Moodle sites you use it on, which is the same as using those sites yourself.

To check for Moodle updates, it sends your site's Moodle version number and branch to download.moodle.org, the same check Moodle runs itself. It sends nothing else about your site or you.

Moonitaur has no server, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.
